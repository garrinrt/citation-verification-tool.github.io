Citation Verifier

Citation Verification Tool

This tool uses two open scholarly indexes, CrossRef and OpenAlex, to help instructors verify sources cited in student work. This is not to be assumed as a definitive tool, but rather one that can help instructors investigate the integrity of student work.

Title Search

Enter the article title, including author and year (if available). This returns the exact published title, the exact journal title, and a working link to the article or abstract.

Sources queried: api.crossref.org · api.openalex.org — both open, public, no key required.
Matching is approximate string similarity on title text; always confirm against the linked record before citing.

Journal Search

Enter the journal and volume, including the issue, (if available). This returns all indexed articles with page ranges for the journal volume, sourced from OpenAlex.

Issue contents are pulled from OpenAlex only, since it supports precise volume/issue filtering; coverage still depends on what's indexed there.
