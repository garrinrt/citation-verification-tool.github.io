Citation Verifier

Citation Verification Tool

This tool uses open scholarly indexes, such as CrossRef and OpenAlex, to help verify the existence of cited resources. This is not to be assumed as a definitive tool, nor used to evaluate work. It is intended to help users locate and confirm cited resources.

Note: not finding a resource with this tool does not confirm the resource does not exist. It only confirms that the open scholarly indexes used in this tool did not locate it.

Title Search

Paste a full citation to search on it directly, or enter a specific field below. This returns the published title, the journal title, and a working link to the article or abstract.

A pasted reference returns a best-guess match. The tool can't confirm it's the same work you pasted — check the returned title, authors, and year against your source before trusting it.

Sources: CrossRef and OpenAlex are queried first; Semantic Scholar, Europe PMC, and the book indexes Google Books and Open Library are checked only when neither returns a strong match. A pasted citation is matched via CrossRef's bibliographic search first. Free full-text links come from Unpaywall, and DOAJ indicates whether the journal is a recognized open-access title. All are open and keyless.

Journal Search

Book Search

Enter a book or edited-volume title, or an ISBN for an exact lookup. This confirms the volume in Google Books and Open Library and lists its publisher, editors, and a link.

This confirms the book or edited volume, not an individual chapter within it. A found book does not verify that a specific cited chapter, its author, or its page range exist.

Book records come from Google Books and Open Library, both open and keyless. An ISBN gives an exact match; a title search is approximate. This confirms the volume, not a chapter within it.
